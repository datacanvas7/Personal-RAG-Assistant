# Northwind Labs - Information Security Policy

*Fictional company and policy, written for the demo dataset of the Personal RAG Assistant.*

## Passwords and sign-in

Every employee must change their password every 90 days. Passwords must have at least
14 characters. Reusing any of the last 10 passwords is not allowed.

Multi-factor authentication is required for the VPN, for e-mail and for the code repository.
Hardware security keys are issued on request to anyone who handles customer data.

## Devices

All laptops use full-disk encryption, which IT enables before the laptop is handed out.
USB storage devices are disabled by default. A manager can ask the IT service desk to enable
one for a specific task, and the exception expires after 30 days.

A lost or stolen device must be reported within 4 hours to security@northwind.example. The
report is needed even if the device was encrypted, because IT has to revoke its access.

## Reporting incidents

Suspicious e-mails are forwarded to phishing@northwind.example, never answered. Anyone who
clicked a link in a suspicious e-mail should say so immediately; nobody is blamed for
reporting quickly.

Security awareness training is mandatory once a year and takes about 45 minutes.
