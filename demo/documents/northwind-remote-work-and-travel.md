# Northwind Labs - Remote Work and Travel

*Fictional company and policy, written for the demo dataset of the Personal RAG Assistant.*

## Working remotely

The default is up to 3 remote days per week. Teams agree on a shared office day. Core hours
are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone: during those hours people are reachable
and meetings may be scheduled.

Employees who work remotely full time can expense a coworking space up to EUR 150 per month.

## Business travel

Book flights in economy class when the flight is shorter than 6 hours; longer flights may be
booked in premium economy. The hotel limit is EUR 140 per night, or EUR 190 in the cities
listed on the finance intranet.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. Claims submitted later need
approval from the head of finance.

Travelling employees receive a daily meal allowance of EUR 35, which is paid without receipts.
