# Northwind Labs - Leave and Benefits

*Fictional company and policy, written for the demo dataset of the Personal RAG Assistant.*

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused
days can be carried over into the next year and must be taken by 31 March; days beyond 5
expire.

## Sick leave

Employees report sick leave to their manager on the first morning. A doctor's note is needed
once the absence lasts more than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay. The second parent
receives 6 weeks at full pay. Leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within
the child's first year.

## Learning and equipment

Each employee has a learning budget of EUR 1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences.
New employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of EUR 400 for a desk, chair or monitor.
Learning budget that is not used does not roll over.
